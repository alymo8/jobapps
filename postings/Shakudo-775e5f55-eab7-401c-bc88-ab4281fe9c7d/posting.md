# Senior Forward Deployed Engineer

## Overview
Shakudo seeks a senior engineer to join their ProServe team, working directly with strategic customers to "turn business problems into production AI and data systems." This role bridges technical implementation and customer outcomes.

## Key Responsibilities
- Embed with customer teams to understand business workflows and data systems
- Translate customer needs into technical architectures and implementation plans
- Build AI/data applications including RAG systems, agentic workflows, and data pipelines
- Deploy and operate Shakudo across complex environments (cloud, hybrid, on-prem, air-gapped)
- Manage production support including incident response and troubleshooting
- Convert customer-specific work into reusable patterns and product feedback

## Required Qualifications
- 8+ years in software, data, platform, infrastructure, or AI engineering
- 3+ years building LLM/AI applications (RAG, agents, evaluations)
- 5+ years with Kubernetes and cloud-native infrastructure
- Strong data engineering background (pipelines, orchestration, transformation)
- Experience in regulated industries (financial services, healthcare, government, etc.)
- Proficiency in Python, Go, TypeScript, Java, or Scala
- Excellent customer-facing communication skills
- Willingness to travel to customer sites

## Preferred Experience
- Forward-deployed or solutions architect roles
- Outcome-based customer engagements
- On-prem, private cloud, or regulated deployments
- Enterprise security and compliance expertise
