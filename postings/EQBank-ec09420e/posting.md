# Forward Deployed AI Engineer

## Position Title
Forward Deployed AI Engineer (Staff-level)

## Location
Toronto, Canada | Hybrid work arrangement

## Department
AI COE | Full-time commitment

## Key Responsibilities

The role focuses on hands-on engineering with design leadership. Primary duties include:

- **Application Development:** "Design, develop, and deploy AI-powered applications and workflows" across backend services, APIs, and enterprise integrations
- **System Architecture:** Create scalable designs balancing delivery speed, reliability, and operational efficiency
- **AI Integration:** Embed LLM capabilities into products and business processes through agent workflows and orchestration
- **Stakeholder Collaboration:** Work directly with business teams to translate requirements into working solutions within weeks
- **Governance:** Implement secure AI systems with prompt safety, injection prevention, and compliance controls

## Required Qualifications

Candidates should demonstrate:

- Proven ability to ship production systems at scale
- Strong backend development and cloud-native expertise (Azure preferred)
- Hands-on experience building LLM applications in production environments
- System design skills balancing practical trade-offs
- "Bias toward shipping and learning from production usage"
- Ownership mentality with end-to-end accountability

## Technology Stack

- Cloud Platform: Microsoft Azure
- AI Models: Claude and enterprise-approved LLMs
- Architecture approach: API-first, event-driven, modular services
