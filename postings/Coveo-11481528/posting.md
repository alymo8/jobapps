## Position Overview

The Forward Deployed Engineer role involves building rapid prototypes and technical proofs for customer use cases. The FDE translates business requirements into scalable solutions, validates feasibility through experimentation, and collaborates with technical teams to create reusable solution patterns.

## Key Responsibilities

- Refine customer needs to shape compelling use cases and solutions
- Develop working prototypes and technical proofs for customer initiatives
- Translate business requirements into functional, testable solutions
- Validate feasibility and performance through hands-on experimentation
- Iterate quickly toward production readiness
- Collaborate with Technical Engagement Managers and Success Architects
- Generate reusable solution patterns across teams
- Bridge business context and technical execution

## Required Qualifications

- 7+ years hands-on software engineering, system integration, or related field experience
- Strong prototyping abilities across APIs, data pipelines, and full-stack systems
- Experience in ambiguous, high-impact environments requiring rapid iteration
- Strong problem-solving and experimentation-driven mindset
- Track record of delivering technical solutions in fast-moving environments

## Preferred Qualifications

- Direct customer-facing technical and solution-oriented experience
- Ability to bridge business context and technical execution
- Curiosity to explore new approaches and expand product capabilities
- Drive to influence product or platform evolution
- Experience pushing product boundaries and innovation
