**Applied AI Engineer, Internal Products**

**Location:** Remote across Canada

**Level:** Mid-level

**About the Role**

You'll be designing and shipping real systems — agents, tools, and workflows that help internal teams across BioRender work more effectively. This role combines hands-on AI engineering with the ability to directly impact how our own organization operates.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Build AI-powered systems and agents that solve real internal product challenges
- Design and implement AI workflows that integrate with existing business tools and processes
- Work with LLMs and modern AI frameworks to ship production-quality systems
- Collaborate with internal teams to understand needs and iterate on solutions
- Contribute to improving development velocity through AI tooling

**Required Qualifications**

- 4+ years of software engineering experience building and shipping production systems or internal applications end-to-end
- Proficiency in Python and JavaScript/Node
- Hands-on experience with AI models and APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic/Claude, Google/Gemini)
- Strong API integration and SaaS tool experience
- Self-directed work style with high agency
- Experience working remotely in distributed teams

**What We're Looking For**

- Strong fundamentals in software engineering and system design
- Ability to move quickly and ship quality code
- Comfort working with cutting-edge AI tools and models
- Experience with prompt engineering and AI model evaluation
- Proactive approach to problem-solving and technical decision-making

**Why Join BioRender**

BioRender is transforming how science is communicated. Our platform helps researchers, educators, and innovators turn complex ideas into clear, compelling scientific visuals — accelerating discovery across academia and industry. Millions of scientists worldwide use BioRender to bring their science to life.

We're on a mission that matters — our figures have appeared in 54,000+ publications, helping scientists share breakthroughs faster and more clearly. Backed by Y Combinator and Dimension Capital at a $900M valuation and profitable since year one, we have the foundation to keep scaling. We're remote-first, offering real flexibility and a connected, supportive culture.
