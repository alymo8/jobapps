# Senior Forward Deployed Engineer

## Position Overview
Unit8, a Swiss AI and data analytics firm, is hiring a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer for their Montreal expansion. This hybrid role focuses on designing robust data solutions and infrastructure for enterprise clients.

## Key Responsibilities
The role involves "developing, deploying, and troubleshooting complex data pipelines and processing systems" with emphasis on Palantir Foundry. You'd collaborate across teams while optimizing data quality and driving client implementations.

## Required Qualifications
- Minimum 5 years in software/data engineering or data science
- Deep expertise in one domain: Data Engineering, Data Science, Cloud Engineering, or GenAI
- Proven pre-sales or technical advisory experience
- Strong Python and/or PySpark skills
- Client-facing communication abilities
- Willingness to travel minimum 10% to client sites

## Preferred Experience
- Palantir Foundry proficiency
- LLM/GenAI solution development background
- Data consulting and architecture design expertise
- Montreal location or willingness to relocate

## What's Offered
The company provides 20 paid days annually, yearly profit sharing reaching "up to 20% of your base salary," and the opportunity to establish Unit8's Canadian presence with significant autonomy and influence over market strategy.
