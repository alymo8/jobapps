# Senior AI Developer, Agentic AI

## Job Title

## Company

## Description
The role involves designing and deploying "production-grade AI solutions that accelerate productivity, innovation, and decision-making." Key responsibilities include architecting agentic AI systems using RAG and multi-agent workflows, integrating with enterprise APIs and Azure services, implementing CI/CD practices, and mentoring junior engineers.

## Key Responsibilities
- Architect and deploy agentic AI systems using RAG and multi-agent workflows
- Integrate AI solutions with enterprise APIs and Azure services
- Implement CI/CD practices for AI/ML systems
- Mentor junior engineers on AI development practices
- Design production-grade AI solutions that drive productivity and innovation

## Key Requirements
- 5+ years in software development, AI, or data engineering
- Strong Python skills and LLM orchestration experience (LangChain/LangGraph)
- Azure AI Foundry proficiency
- Bilingual (French/English)
