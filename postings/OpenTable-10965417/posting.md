## Position Overview

Build and deploy production-ready AI applications at the intersection of business strategy and technology. The role focuses on building end-to-end AI-powered solutions, workflow automations, and intelligent data systems that drive measurable business impact.

## Key Responsibilities

- **Business Discovery:** Partner with strategy, sales, and operations stakeholders to understand how their workflows function, and proactively identify opportunities where AI-driven solutions can create impact
- **AI Development:** Build and deploy custom AI applications including LLM integrations, RAG pipelines, agentic workflows, and automated triggers
- **System Integration:** Integrate AI systems with Snowflake, APIs, and third-party tools
- **Process Optimization:** Identify and automate recurring manual tasks and operational bottlenecks
- **Quality Control:** Implement evaluation frameworks and monitoring systems for reliability and accuracy
- **Stakeholder Communication:** Articulate technical concepts and solutions to non-technical partners
- **End-to-End Ownership:** Own projects from discovery through deployment

## Required Qualifications

- 5+ years in software engineering, data engineering, or applied AI
- Bachelor's or Master's in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, or related field
- Hands-on experience with OpenAI/Anthropic APIs, LangChain, LlamaIndex, vector databases
- Strong Python and SQL/Snowflake proficiency
- Experience with Git, API design, data orchestration tools (Airflow), and deployment
- Excellent communication skills across technical and non-technical teams
- Ability to think strategically about business problems and technical solutions
- Self-directed work with ambiguous requirements
