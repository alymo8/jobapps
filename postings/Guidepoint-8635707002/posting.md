## Job Description

Guidepoint is hiring an experienced AI Engineer for their Toronto Technology Hub. The role focuses on building scalable generative AI systems, LLM applications, and research agents while implementing responsible AI practices and MLOps excellence.

### Key Responsibilities
- Design and operate production-grade backend services and APIs for generative AI features, including RAG pipelines
- Manage the complete lifecycle of AI applications from design through deployment and monitoring
- Optimize retrieval-augmented generation systems through advanced techniques
- Integrate LLMs with proprietary knowledge repositories and real-time data streams
- Establish LLMOps best practices and evaluation frameworks
- Mentor junior engineers and shape product direction

### Required Qualifications

**Education & Experience:**
- Bachelor's in Computer Science/Engineering with 6+ years professional experience, OR Master's with 4+ years
- 2+ years building production LLM applications (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini)

**Technical Skills:**
- Python, FastAPI/Flask, asynchronous programming (asyncio)
- Cloud platforms (Azure preferred; AWS/GCP acceptable)
- Docker, Kubernetes, Helm
- RAG, vector databases, multi-agent systems
- Databricks/Apache Spark
- LangChain or LlamaIndex frameworks

**Soft Skills:**
- Leadership and mentorship capabilities
- Complex project management experience
