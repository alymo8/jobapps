## Posting

## Who We Are

Honeycomb.io is a Series D funded company with 200+ employees building the observability platform for modern software. We believe that every company needs to understand what's happening inside their systems, and we're building tools that make that possible at scale.

## The Role

We're looking for a Senior Software Engineer II to build production-grade AI agents that investigate and act on observability data within Canvas, Honeycomb's agentic workspace.

You'll own the complete lifecycle of agent development: from prototype to production deployment and beyond. You'll design agents that leverage Honeycomb's unique high-cardinality data store to deliver insights and actions that would be impossible on lower-quality data. You'll work alongside product, infrastructure, and research teams to shape the future of how AI interacts with observability data.

## What You'll Do

- Design and deliver trustworthy agents for live observability data, from prototype to production
- Own the complete lifecycle: scope, build, ship, maintain, and evaluate agents
- Leverage Honeycomb's high-cardinality data store to build agents with unique reasoning capabilities
- Ship new features to Canvas, the MCP server, and Canvas Skills
- Establish quality standards for agent performance and reliability
- Work with cross-functional teams to translate business goals into agent capabilities

## What We're Looking For

- **Shipped LLM-based production systems** - Not research demos or proofs-of-concept, but real systems serving real users
- **End-to-end ownership** - You own problems completely without handoff dependencies
- **Current judgment about recent shifts in agent architecture and design** - You're tracking how the field is evolving (last 6-12 months)
- **Deep understanding of how fast data stores enable novel agent reasoning** - You understand the unique advantages Honeycomb's architecture provides
- **Product vision** - You can prototype quickly to validate ideas, not just propose them

## Preferred Experience

- Observability or developer-tools background
- Familiarity with eval frameworks, agent tooling, RAG, and prompt engineering
- Experience building agents that reason over large, structured datasets
