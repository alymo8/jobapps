## Job Description

Cresta seeks an AI Agent Engineer to develop and deploy AI agents addressing real customer challenges. The role combines engineering expertise with customer engagement, focusing on "leveraging the latest technologies in Large Language Models and AI Agent systems" while ensuring cost-effectiveness and reliability.

### Key Responsibilities

- Develop and optimize AI agents using Cresta's platform
- Build integrations with external systems (APIs, databases, CRMs)
- Troubleshoot and optimize performance in enterprise environments
- Collaborate with customers on requirements gathering and solution design
- Conduct demos and proof-of-concept presentations
- Coordinate cross-functional projects with internal teams

### Required Qualifications

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field
- 3+ years in software development, consulting, AI/ML engineering, or forward deployed engineering
- Proficiency in Python and Golang
- Familiarity with LLMs, prompt engineering, and AI agent frameworks
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure) and DevOps practices
- Strong API integration and communication skills
